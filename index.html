Questions

Before you run a report.

Where does the data come from?

YachtWay's market team monitors boat listings across the industry, every builder, model and region. VATO turns that into 109,114 tracked listings, checked and refreshed every week.

How is the number built?

From the comparable listings, adjusted for engine hours and condition. Every adjustment is shown in the PDF, so the owner can check it.

Can I send the report to clients?

Yes. Every report is a PDF. Email it to the owner, hand it to your buyer, or attach it to a loan file.

Who on my team can use it?

Everyone with a seat. Each person signs in with their own email, and the whole organization shares one plan.

What does it cost?

Your first 5 reports are free, shared across the team. For plans, contact YachtWay.
